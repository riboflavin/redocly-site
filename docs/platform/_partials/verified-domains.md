Verified domains are a way for you to connect users with a given organization.
They only apply to corporate identity providers when logging into the Redocly platform, not logging in to projects.
You can add a Verified domain on the **SSO and login** page in Redocly.

When you add a Verified domain to your organization, users logging in to Redocly with the verified domain email are directed to corporate identity providers.
Users keep the option to use Redocly credentials or social logins.
If you also select to require SSO authentication, that option is removed.
Users with the verified domain email can only log in using corporate identity providers.
